## Bhilwara

- Station: BHL (Bhilwara railway station, North Western Railway, Ajmer division, on the Ajmer–Chittaurgarh section of the Jaipur–Ratlam line)
- Commercial airport: none
- Highways:
  - NH 48 (Golden Quadrilateral: Delhi–Jaipur–Kishangarh–Gulabpura–Bhilwara–Chittaurgarh–Udaipur–Ahmedabad–Mumbai). The Kishangarh–Gulabpura and Gulabpura–Chittorgarh stretches are six-laned. The highway skirts Bhilwara city, and traffic enters town on SH 12. This is the main road from Delhi, Jaipur, Ajmer, Kishangarh, Udaipur and Ahmedabad.
  - NH 758 (Rajsamand–Kunwariya–Gangapur–Bhilwara–Ladpura) runs through Bhilwara city and meets NH 27 at Ladpura. It is the shortest road from Udaipur (via NH 58/NH 758) and the road that brings Kota traffic in from Ladpura.
  - NH 27 (East-West Corridor, formerly NH 76: Chittaurgarh–Kota) crosses south-eastern Bhilwara district near Ladpura and Bijolia, not the city itself. Drivers from Kota take NH 27 and then NH 758.
  - NH 148D (Bhim–Gulabpura–Shahpura–Jahazpur–Hindoli–Uniara) crosses the north of the district and meets NH 48 at Gulabpura.
- Buses:
  - Bhilwara has a government RSRTC (Rajasthan Roadways) bus depot in the heart of the city, and Bhilwara appears on RSRTC's list of major depots. Many private operators also serve the city.
  - Jaipur–Bhilwara: RSRTC buses leave from Sindhi Camp, Jaipur's central RSRTC bus stand. The trip takes about 5 to 6.5 hours, and non-AC fares start at about Rs 285–380 (AbhiBus RSRTC listing; Cleartrip estimates about 5 h).
  - Udaipur–Bhilwara: RSRTC and private buses run through the day, with listed departures from about 1 AM to about 11:30 PM. The trip takes about 3 to 3.5 hours. RSRTC buses leave from Udaipur's city bus stand (AbhiBus, redBus, MakeMyTrip).
  - Delhi–Bhilwara: private AC sleeper buses take about 8 to 10 hours, run by operators such as Rishabh Travels, R.S. Yadav Travels, Ashok Travels (Mandsaur Group), Shrinath Travel Agency and Chaudhary Bus Service (via.com, Paytm, AbhiBus).
  - Ahmedabad–Bhilwara: many AC sleeper buses run, typically leaving in the evening and arriving after midnight, in about 8 to 9 hours (via.com lists Gill Travels at about 8h 15m and Chirag Travels at about 9 h). Paytm and AbhiBus also list several operators.
  - Indore–Bhilwara: AC sleeper buses take about 6.5 to 8.5 hours. Operators include Intercity, Ashok Travels (Mandsaur Group), Chouhan Travels and Gagan Travels; via.com lists about 15 to 19 buses a day.
  - Kota–Bhilwara: MakeMyTrip lists a direct bus taking about 3h 50m.

## Verifier notes

Method: I re-ran every road distance on two independent OpenStreetMap routing engines, OSRM and Valhalla, from each station or airport (with OSM/Nominatim coordinates) to Bhilwara railway station (25.3452 N, 74.6315 E). I then compared the results with booking and route sites not in the earlier source list where possible. km values are rounded to the nearest 5, and drive times are conservative car estimates. The two engines agreed within about 3 km on every leg except Jaipur airport (252 vs 261 km).

CORRECTIONS TO THE PREVIOUS DATASET
1. Vande Bharat removed. The Udaipur City–Jaipur Vande Bharat (20979/20980) was withdrawn in February 2026 for low occupancy. Wikipedia lists the last service as 13 Feb 2026; NWR and Outlook Traveller give 14 Feb. Remove every Vande Bharat claim: Jaipur in 3h 50m, Ajmer in 1h 45m and the Udaipur listing. The fastest Bhilwara–Jaipur train is now about 4h 10m.
2. Chittaurgarh: the statement that every through train calls at both stations is false. erail shows 12 of 40 trains running via Chanderiya (CNA) instead of Chittaurgarh Jn.
3. Kota: only one direct train, the weekly 22176 (Fridays), stops at Kota Jn. The other 4 stop at Sogaria. The claim that 22175/22176 runs Fridays only is confirmed for 22176 only.
4. Ratlam: 4 daily trains are confirmed (19346, 17606, 14801, 19711). 12719 is not daily, so I replaced it with 19346.
5. Jaipur airport: changed from 250 to 255 km. OSRM gives 252, closestairportto 253.8, MakeMyTrip 253 and Valhalla 261.
6. Train counts are now from erail: COR 40 (28 to COR), AII 39, UDZ 13, JP 22, RTM 22, KOTA 5 (1 to Kota Jn). The RailYatri counts used earlier were lower and out of date.
7. Bus facts: I dropped the exact redBus counts (32 Jaipur options, 18 Udaipur services, 27/40 Delhi buses) and the FlixBus claim. They are snapshots that change daily, and I could not confirm them independently. I kept only facts confirmed on at least two aggregators or an official or encyclopedic source.

ORDERING (CLOSE CALLS)
- Airports: Udaipur UDR 145 < Kishangarh KQH 160 < Jaipur JAI 255 < Ahmedabad AMD 410. Kishangarh vs Udaipur airport is not close: both engines give a 15–19 km gap. Wikipedia's figures (Udaipur airport 165 km, Kishangarh 130 km) are outliers.
- Junctions: Chittaurgarh 55 < Ajmer 135 < Udaipur City 155 < Kota 160 < Jaipur 250 < Ratlam 265.
- Udaipur vs Kota is a genuine close call. Station to station, both engines put Udaipur City at about 156 km and Kota Jn at about 161 km, so Udaipur comes first. City centre to city centre, Kota (about 153 km) is slightly nearer than Udaipur (about 155 km), because Kota Jn sits in north Kota. Because the list is of railway stations, Udaipur stays ahead of Kota. In guest copy, describing both as 'about 155–160 km' avoids any dispute.
- Jaipur Jn (248–249 km) and Ratlam (265–271 km) are clearly ordered.

AIRPORT FACTS
- Bhilwara has no commercial airport. The Hamirgarh airstrip (Takhtpura, about 19 km away) has no scheduled flights: a Lok Sabha reply of Dec 2024 says no UDAN routes were awarded through five bidding rounds.
- Udaipur (UDR) is the nearest airport with good connections.
- Kishangarh (KQH) has only IndiGo and Star Air, at low frequency. 2026 listings show routes to Ahmedabad, Noida/Hindon, Nagpur and Lucknow. A 2025 report says the airport operates only from sunrise to sunset, so guests should check schedules.
- Jaipur (JAI) has the widest choice of flights. Ahmedabad (AMD) is the furthest of the four.

COMBINED ROUTES FOR THE GUEST MESSAGE (all verified)
- Fly to Udaipur, then about 2.5 h by cab, about 3–3.5 h by RSRTC or private bus, or about 3–5 h by train from Udaipur City.
- Fly to Kishangarh, then about 2.5 h by cab. Trains also run from Kishangarh and Ajmer.
- Fly to Jaipur, then about 4 h by cab, about 4–5.5 h by train from Jaipur Jn, or about 5–6.5 h by RSRTC bus from Sindhi Camp.
- Fly to Ahmedabad, then about 7 h by cab or an overnight AC sleeper bus of about 8–9 h. The direct 12982 Asarva (Ahmedabad)–Jaipur SF Express runs daily overnight via Udaipur and stops at Bhilwara (erail, Goibibo).
- Self-drive: NH 48 from Delhi, Jaipur, Ajmer and Kishangarh, and from Ahmedabad via Udaipur and Chittaurgarh. NH 758 from Udaipur/Rajsamand (via Gangapur) and from Kota (via NH 27 to Ladpura).
- Long-distance buses: direct AC sleepers from Delhi (about 8–10 h), Ahmedabad (about 8–9 h) and Indore (about 6.5–8.5 h), plus frequent RSRTC services from Jaipur, Udaipur and Kota.

Caveats: train running days and bus timetables come from aggregators (erail, etrain, RailMitra, AbhiBus, via.com, Paytm). Guests should confirm on NTES or IRCTC and on RSRTC or booking apps for the December 2026 dates.
