# Save the Train: pre-invite plan for your friends

*Only for your friends and personal connections. The family is inviting relatives separately.*

---

## 1. The clock

**Rule (confirmed):** booking opens **60 days before the journey date, at 8:00 AM IST**. The journey day itself isn't counted. Long-distance trains count from the date the train **leaves its starting station**, so friends who board partway may find booking opened a day earlier. That last point comes from secondary sources that agree with each other. I found no official circular that states it.

| Journey date | Booking opens (8:00 AM IST) |
|---|---|
| Sun 6 Dec | Wed 7 Oct (already open; matters only for 2–3 day journeys) |
| Mon 7 Dec | **Thu 8 Oct (today)** |
| Tue 8 Dec | **Fri 9 Oct (tomorrow)** |
| Wed 9 Dec | **Sat 10 Oct** (probably the main arrival day) |
| Thu 10 Dec | Sun 11 Oct (station counters usually open only 8 AM–2 PM on Sundays) |
| Fri 11 Dec | Mon 12 Oct |
| Sat 12 Dec | Tue 13 Oct (probably the main return day) |
| Sun 13 Dec | Wed 14 Oct |
| Mon 14 Dec | Thu 15 Oct |
| Tatkal (backup) | The day before the train leaves its starting station: 10 AM for AC, 11 AM for non-AC. Max 4 people per booking. Online Tatkal needs an Aadhaar-verified IRCTC account and an Aadhaar OTP. |

**The four tips friends most need:**
1. **Aadhaar-verify your IRCTC account tonight** (My Account → Authenticate User or Aadhaar KYC → OTP to the mobile linked to your Aadhaar). *Reported, not officially confirmed:* since 12 Jan 2026, online booking on the opening day (8 AM to midnight) is reportedly limited to Aadhaar-verified accounts. Everyone else can book online only from the next day, or at a station counter. The source is Deccan Chronicle quoting IRCTC; I found no official notice.
2. **Be ready at 7:50 AM.** Save passengers in the IRCTC Master List and keep UPI or the IRCTC eWallet ready. One booking holds **up to 6 people**, so one friend can book for the whole gang. Tick **VIKALP** (alternate trains) and pick 2 backup trains.
3. **Book now and cancel later if needed.** *Reported, from April 2026 news coverage:* cancelling more than 72 hours before departure costs only a flat clerkage fee. A fully waitlisted e-ticket is cancelled automatically and can't be used to board a reserved coach. The minimum fine has been ₹500 plus fare since 20 Jun 2026.
4. **Book the return on its own opening day:** 12 Dec returns open 13 Oct, and 13 Dec returns open 14 Oct. One person per booking must carry an **original** photo ID. The e-ticket on a phone is enough.

**Left out on purpose:** the counter Tatkal token system (reported only in West Central Railway), waitlist cap percentages and the 24-hour chart claims. On the page, word rules as "check IRCTC/RailOne or call 139", not as guarantees.

**Timing problem:** the website can't arrive before the 9 Oct opening and gives almost no warning for 10 Oct. **Send a plain WhatsApp booking alert tonight**, separate from the RSVP.

## 2. Recommended concept: "Save the Train"

The pre-invite is a train ticket to your wedding, with each friend's name printed on it.

1. **Tonight (8 Oct):** send a plain 1:1 WhatsApp. Include the dates, city and arrival station, "journey date minus 60 days, 8 AM", "Aadhaar-verify IRCTC tonight", and "RSVP link coming tomorrow".
2. **9 Oct evening:** send each friend a personal message through a pre-filled WhatsApp link, plus a 15–20 second voice note from the two of you. If you build the preview add-on, the preview reads "Rahul, your ticket to S & V's wedding".
3. **They open the link (no login).** The ticket page greets them by name, shows your one personal line, and puts a countdown at the top ("Your 9 Dec booking opens in 11h 40m"). Below it is a short "What's covered" strip.
4. **Status (5 seconds).** *CNF: I'm in* / *RAC: very likely* (pick "I'll confirm by" 20 or 31 Oct, plus a reason: leave, budget or date clash) / *Can't make it* (opens a short message box for the couple).
5. **Party (10 seconds).** Just me / + partner (name already filled in) / + plus-one (only if you allowed one) / number of kids.
6. **From (10 seconds).** City from a suggestion list, plus how they'll travel: Train, Flight, Road or Local.
7. **Dates (20 seconds).** Arrival date (7–13 Dec) plus time of day, departure date plus time of day. "Not sure" is allowed.
8. **Room (10 seconds).** Own room / share with friends / with my partner / staying with my own people / local. Each option says what it means in plain words. There's an optional box for "who would you like to share with?".
9. **Confirm ticket.** The page flips to a boarding pass they can screenshot, with "Edit anytime from this same link".
10. **After submitting (all optional).** Their booking-open times for onward and return trips with a Google Calendar 7:50 AM alarm, an IRCTC checklist, a share-to-gang button and a leave-email generator.
11. **12–15 Oct:** the same link unlocks "I've booked: add my train".

The RSVP itself (steps 4–8) takes about **60 seconds**.

## 3. Idea menu

**Core (ship by 10 Oct)**
- **Tonight's booking alert:** plain 1:1 WhatsApp. It's the only thing that can go out before the opening days.
- **Personal links + private guest list:** a random code per friend, plus one general link for anyone you forget. The form never asks for anything you already know.
- **Ticket-style RSVP page:** one screen, and the same link reopens to edit. Answers go straight into your Google Sheet.
- **"Confirm by" date instead of Maybe:** turns "will confirm later" into a date you can follow up on, with a reason.
- **Room question:** one tap. Combined with party size and dates, it tells you rooms and nights.
- **Countdown + calendar alarm + IRCTC checklist:** puts the urgency at the top of the page.
- **"What's covered" card:** budget-conscious friends answer differently depending on who pays for the stay.
- **WhatsApp send kit:** a local list of pre-filled 1:1 messages, about 20 seconds per friend. No broadcast list.

**Delight add-ons (pick 1–3)**
- **Name in the link preview:** a small static page per friend, so the WhatsApp preview shows their name. Biggest delight for the effort.
- **Leave kit:** "Formal" or "Honest" leave email plus a working-days count. Office friends are likely to screenshot it.
- **Share-to-gang button:** "Booking at 8 AM, who's in? Up to 6 per booking". It must share the general link, never the friend's personal one.
- **Roommate picker:** first names from the friend's own gang, showing mutual matches only. Ship it only if the core is tested by 6 PM on 9 Oct.
- **Gang tally board:** "7 of 12 in", counts only, one board per gang.
- **Playlist + sangeet squad sign-up:** gives a choreographer 8 weeks with the volunteers.

**Phase 2 (after booking)**
- **"I've booked" travel card (12–15 Oct):** train or flight number, station, time, PNR optional, pickup needed. This is the source of named arrival and departure times.
- **Reminder list (from 12 Oct):** a daily list of pre-written WhatsApp messages, at most 2 reminders per friend.
- **Room planning (early Nov):** a rooms × nights grid with early check-in and late checkout flags. A rough version can go in the Sheet within 2–3 days of sending.
- **Arrivals board + pickup run sheets (by 25 Nov):** grouped by station and time window.
- **"Plans changed" quick buttons + change alerts to you (late Oct):** lock edits around 25 Nov.
- **Friends travelling from the same city (squads):** useful once there are responses. Captains collect age and gender over WhatsApp, not on the site.
- **Couple quiz, departure board, story timeline:** hooks for November and a welcome screen at the venue.
- **Shareable boarding-pass image:** only after the family's invites are out; Close Friends only, no venue.
- **One-click exports (mid-Nov):** includes a one-row summary to hand to the family.

**Cut**
- **Group codes (Coach Code):** duplicates the gang tags you already have, and lets uninvited people add themselves.
- **QR pass + scanner desk:** the pickup crew needs a run sheet, not a scanner.
- **Google Form or Tally as the main tool:** answers can't be edited and get duplicated. Keep them as fallbacks only.
- **Supabase or Firebase:** overkill. Data doesn't land in a Sheet, one loose setting leaks everyone's details, and Supabase pauses after 7 idle days.
- **Glide, Softr or Notion:** friends would have to sign in to edit their own RSVP.
- **WhatsApp Business API:** approvals take too long, it costs money, and messages look like spam to friends.
- **WhatsApp group Events or polls as the RSVP:** creates a second, conflicting RSVP. Use them only to pin the link.

## 4. What we capture

**Step 1 (now)**
- **Already known from the guest list:** name, gang, plus-one rule, partner name.
- **Asked:**
  - status (In / Likely with confirm-by date and reason / No)
  - party (partner or plus-one name, kids count)
  - city and how they'll travel
  - arrival date and time of day
  - departure date and time of day
  - room need
  - optional roommate wish and note
- **Added automatically:** time, version, which link they used, first opened.
- **Never asked:** phone number, Aadhaar, ages.

**Deferred to Step 2:** for each leg, train or flight number, boarding and arrival station, scheduled time, ticket status, PNR (optional, stays in the private Sheet), who they're travelling with, pickup or drop needed, and diet.

**How it adds up**
- **Headcount:** for each friend's latest answer marked In, count 1 + partner or plus-one + kids. Count "Likely" at about 0.6 for an early hotel hold, and switch to "Booked" for final numbers.
- **Rooms × nights:** a person needs night D if they arrive by that night and leave on D+1 or later. Rooms per night ≈ couples + own-room singles + (sharers ÷ 2.5). Flag arrivals before about 11 AM (early check-in or an extra night) and departures after about 2 PM (late checkout, or one shared freshen-up room).
- **Arrivals and departures list:** Step 1 gives a provisional date and time of day per name. Step 2 adds train number, station and time, sorted by station into 60–90 minute pickup windows.

## 5. Build options

| Option | Effort | Trade-offs |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Claude Code page on Cloudflare Pages + Google Apps Script + Google Sheet (recommended)** | 6–8 h, ₹0 | No login, the same link edits, tracks who opened, and data is ready for all three of your goals. Catches: deploy as "Execute as me, Anyone"; send the data as plain text with no custom headers; publish a new version after every code change; 1–3 second first load; Google shows *you* (not guests) an "unverified app" warning once. |
| **B. Same page, saving to a hidden Google Form** | +1 h swap | Fewer setup traps, but it can't read answers back, failed saves are silently lost, and every edit adds a duplicate row. Switch to this if A fails a real-phone test by 6 PM on 9 Oct. |
| **C. Pre-filled Google Form links (or Tally)** | ~2 h | Looks generic, edit links get lost, duplicates. Emergency only, if the page isn't ready by 10 PM on 9 Oct. Never turn on "Limit to 1 response" or "View results summary". |

**Hosting:** Cloudflare Pages direct upload. A free GitHub Pages site needs a *public* repo, so never commit the guest list, codes or phone numbers there. Netlify's free plan can pause your site after about 20 deploys. Test inside WhatsApp's in-app browser on both Android and iPhone.

## 6. Open questions for you

1. **City and stations:** wedding city, main arrival station(s), nearby alternative stations and the airport. Tonight's alert depends on this.
2. **Events and nights:** which events are on 10 and 11 Dec, and which nights (9, 10, 11 Dec) you expect friends to stay.
3. **What you cover:** stay (which nights, twin or triple sharing), meals, pickups and local transport. If the stay isn't covered, the group rate and its booking deadline.
4. **Plus-ones:** partners only, an open +1, or kids? Decided per friend.
5. **Size:** roughly how many friends, and which gangs (college, school, work).
6. **Deadline:** the hotel's room-release date, with the "confirm by" deadline set about a week before it (20 or 31 Oct).
7. **Style and overlap:** the look you want (ticket or retro railway?), whether any public sharing is OK, and which of you or the family "owns" friends who are also relatives, so nobody gets counted or given a room twice.
